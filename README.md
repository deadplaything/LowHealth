# LowHealth
Final Fantasy 11 Addon 
LowHealth

Party awareness when you don't have time to stare at six HP bars.

LowHealth is a lightweight Windower 4 addon for Final Fantasy XI that provides immediate visual and audio alerts for critical party conditions.

Whether you're healing, tanking, multiboxing, or just trying to keep track of a chaotic fight, LowHealth puts the information that matters in front of you.

Features
Low HP alerts with configurable thresholds
Priority-player monitoring with a separate warning threshold
Party-list heart indicators
Death detection with visual and audio alerts
Critical debuff monitoring:
Doom
Petrification
Sleep
Paralysis
Poison
Individual sounds for each condition
Optional sound packs, including spoken debuff alerts
Draggable and scalable alerts
Configurable party-list icons
Built-in settings interface
Designed to remain lightweight during combat
Know what happened before you have to look.

LowHealth isn't meant to play the game for you. It simply makes important party information much harder to miss.
