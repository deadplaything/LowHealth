# LowHealth
Final Fantasy 11 Addon

### Party awareness when you don't have time to stare at six HP bars.

**LowHealth** is a lightweight **Windower 4 addon for Final Fantasy XI** that provides immediate visual and audio alerts for critical party conditions.

Whether you're healing, tanking, multiboxing, or just trying to keep track of a chaotic fight, **LowHealth puts the information that matters in front of you.**

---

## Features

- **Low HP Alerts**
  - Configurable HP warning threshold
  - Audio and visual warnings
  - Multiple low-health party members can be displayed at once

- **Priority Player Monitoring**
  - Select a specific party member for additional monitoring
  - Separate configurable HP threshold
  - Dedicated priority warning sound
  - Great for keeping extra attention on your tank or another critical party member

- **Party List Indicators**
  - Heart icon for party status
  - Skull indicator when a party member dies
  - Critical status-effect icons
  - Adjustable icon size and positioning

- **Death Detection**
  - Immediate visual death alert
  - Dedicated death sound
  - Zone/load protection to prevent false death alerts while party members are zoning

- **Critical Debuff Monitoring**
  - Doom
  - Petrification
  - Sleep
  - Paralysis
  - Poison

- **Individual Status Sounds**
  - Each monitored debuff can have its own sound
  - Priority HP warning has its own sound
  - Death has its own sound
  - Normal low-health warnings have their own sound

- **Custom Sound Support**
  - Replace the included `.wav` files with your own sounds
  - Alternate sound packs can provide completely different alert styles
  - Spoken alerts can announce conditions such as **"DOOM!"**, **"SLEEP!"**, or **"PARALYSIS!"**

- **Customizable Interface**
  - Draggable alerts
  - Adjustable alert scaling
  - Adjustable font size
  - Adjustable party icon scaling and positioning
  - Built-in settings interface

---

## Why LowHealth?

FFXI fights can get busy fast.

Between watching mechanics, positioning, buffs, debuffs, timers, equipment, and the party list, it's easy to miss something important.

**LowHealth is designed to make critical party information difficult to miss without playing the game for you.**

It doesn't heal anyone.

It doesn't remove debuffs.

It doesn't make combat decisions.

It simply makes sure you know when something needs your attention.

---

## Sound Packs

LowHealth supports customizable alert sounds.

The default installation includes standard notification sounds, but additional sound packs can be used to completely change how the addon communicates alerts.

Example sound pack ideas:

- **Default**
- **Spoken Alerts**
- **Minimal**
- **Classic FFXI**
- **Retro RPG**
- **Streamer**

A spoken alert pack could announce:

- `"LOW HEALTH"`
- `"PRIORITY LOW"`
- `"DOOM"`
- `"PETRIFICATION"`
- `"SLEEP"`
- `"PARALYSIS"`
- `"POISON"`
- `"PLAYER DOWN"`

Simply replace the corresponding `.wav` files while keeping the expected filenames.
